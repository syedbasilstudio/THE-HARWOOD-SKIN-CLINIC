The Harwood standard

MORE THAN A TREATMENT.
A more considered experience.

Harwood Skin Clinic was built around a simple idea: that expert skin care and genuine comfort should never be a choice between one or the other.

Every appointment begins with a proper conversation. Every treatment is delivered with clinical care, unhurried attention and a room that feels calm the moment you walk in.

The founder

MEET
NATASHA SNAPE.

Before Harwood Skin Clinic, Natasha spent fifteen years working as an NHS Dental Therapist. That period shaped everything about how she works today — a clinical regard for hygiene, a precise hand, an understanding of anatomy and a naturally reassuring way with people who might be nervous.

She opened her own private clinic in Bolton to bring that same standard into skin care: unhurried consultations, treatments chosen for your skin on the day, and a room that feels nothing like a hospital and nothing like a salon.
